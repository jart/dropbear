# dropbear for humans

Download <https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1xs1--iiHBQFqYckefw5xlxvq8E0DUo5I?usp=sharing> to
your `~/equitydata/` folder. This will give you access to the minute bars of many stocks. That way
you can run backtests without needing to pay for access to market data from alpaca or databento.
